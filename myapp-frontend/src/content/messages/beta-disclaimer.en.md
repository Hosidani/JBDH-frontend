# Use of AI

JBDH uses artificial intelligence (AI) to analyse CVs, match candidates to jobs and provide recommendations. It is important to be aware that:

- **AI can be wrong.** All analyses, matches and recommendations should be treated as guidance — not as absolute truth.
- **Always review the results** before making decisions based on them.
- **AI models can be biased** and may reflect patterns from training data that are not always representative.

Use JBDH as one tool among many, not as the sole basis for decisions.
